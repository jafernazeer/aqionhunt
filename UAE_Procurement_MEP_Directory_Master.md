## 📊 Summary of 8 Core Categories & 25 Specialized Trades

### Electrical & Power
*LV/MV electrical installations, distribution boards, lighting controls, and authority testing.*

- **Electrical Installation & Wiring** (`elec-01`)
  - *Scope:* New circuits, rewiring and dedicated supplies for heavy equipment. Cable sizing and load calculations done properly.
  - *Authorities:* DEWA, ADDC, SEWA, FEWA
  - *Typical BOQ Units:* Linear Meters (LM), Points, Circuits
- **Distribution Board Works** (`elec-02`)
  - *Scope:* New boards, upgrades, breaker changes, load balancing across phases, correct labelling and schedules.
  - *Authorities:* DEWA, ADDC, Dubai Civil Defense
  - *Typical BOQ Units:* Units / Panels, Ways (4W, 8W, 12W, 18W, 24W)
- **Lighting Installation** (`elec-03`)
  - *Scope:* Indoor, outdoor and landscape lighting. LED upgrades, dimming and controls.
  - *Authorities:* Dubai Municipality, Estidama, Al Sa'fat
  - *Typical BOQ Units:* Light Points, Fixtures, Linear Meters
- **Testing & Certification** (`elec-04`)
  - *Scope:* Insulation resistance, earth continuity, loop impedance and RCD testing, with written results you can keep.
  - *Authorities:* DEWA, ADDC, SEWA
  - *Typical BOQ Units:* Board / Test Package, Inspection Sign-off

*Potable water supply, HDPE/uPVC drainage, sanitary fixtures, and non-destructive leak detection.*

- **Water Supply Installation** (`plumb-01`)

  - *Typical BOQ Units:* Linear Meters, Water Points, Pump Skids
- **Drainage & Waste Systems** (`plumb-02`)
  - *Scope:* HDPE and uPVC waste, traps and floor drains, including F&B specification work for commercial kitchens.
  - *Authorities:* Dubai Municipality Drainage Dept, Dubai Food Safety Department
  - *Typical BOQ Units:* Drainage Points, Linear Meters, Interceptor Units
- **Taps, Mixers & Sanitaryware** (`plumb-03`)
  - *Scope:* Supply and installation of taps, mixers, showers, WCs, basins and water heaters. Replacing worn or leaking fittings and re-piping where needed.
  - *Authorities:* Dubai Municipality, Al Sa'fat Water Conservation Standards

- **Leak Detection & Repair** (`plumb-04`)

  - *Authorities:* DEWA Leak Assessment, Building Management Systems
