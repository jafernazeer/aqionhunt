   - *URL:* [yello.ae](https://www.yello.ae)

     * `/category/clinics-medical-centres`
     * `/category/law-firms-legal-services`
2. **Clutch.co UAE**:
   - *URL:* [clutch.co/ae](https://clutch.co/ae)
   - *Data Available:* UAE businesses submitting client briefs for AI development, custom chatbots, and voice bots.
3. **GoodFirms UAE**:
   - *URL:* [goodfirms.co](https://www.goodfirms.co/directory/country/top-software-development-companies/uae)
   - *Data Available:* B2B matching and agency project requirement RFQs.
4. **Sortlist UAE & DesignRush UAE**:
   - *URL:* [sortlist.com/agency/united-arab-emirates](https://www.sortlist.com/agency/united-arab-emirates)
   - *Data Available:* GCC corporate briefs matched with AI and marketing automation agencies.
5. **Upwork UAE Client Project Feed**:
   - *URL:* [upwork.com](https://www.upwork.com/nx/search/jobs/?location=United%20Arab%20Emirates&q=AI)
